# Code of Conduct

Obsidian Hermes concerns personal knowledge, automation, and consequential
agent actions. Clear, respectful collaboration and careful handling of safety
concerns are essential.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome newcomers and explain trade-offs without gatekeeping.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections quickly.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Handle security reports, private data, logs, and credentials carefully.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing secrets, exploit details, or vulnerable configurations publicly when
  responsible disclosure is appropriate.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after maintainers ask for a topic to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests;
or temporarily or permanently restrict contributors whose behaviour harms the
project or community.

Report conduct concerns to a repository maintainer through a private channel
when possible. If that is unsafe or unavailable, use
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Report vulnerabilities according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
